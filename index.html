TillFolder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026
Contact: hassan@mapifyit.com

TillFolder is a receipt scanner and expense tracker for Android. You do not need a TillFolder account. We do not operate a server that receives your receipt library.

Your receipts and expenses

Images and PDFs you select, recognised text, shop names, dates, amounts, currencies, notes, folders, budgets and reminders are kept in TillFolder's private app storage. Camera, photo and file access is used for the import you request. TillFolder does not read your bank account, email inbox or entire photo library. Notification permission is requested when you choose to schedule a reminder.

Text recognition and google components

Receipt text recognition runs on the device using Google's ML Kit. The image and recognition results are not sent to Google for that text-recognition operation. Google's SDKs may send technical diagnostics and usage information, including device and app details, performance/error information and installation-scoped identifiers. The enhanced scanner uses Google Play services and may download components. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure and Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy for those components.

Purchases

Optional TillFolder Pro is purchased through Google Play. Google handles checkout and payment details. TillFolder does not receive your card number. The app verifies and stores a Google-signed purchase record locally to remember your Pro access and uses Google Play to restore purchases and check their current status. No custom purchase-verification server is used. Complimentary access stores only a local code hash/access state. This version has no advertising SDK or advertising placements.

Backups, exports and sharing

Backups include your records and original files, including records retained in Recently deleted. Local recovery copies remain on this device. You can export a backup or select a compatible file provider for an ongoing backup destination. A selected provider, such as a cloud storage app, receives the data you send there under its own terms and privacy policy. TillFolder cannot guarantee that a provider has completed cloud synchronization merely because its local file accepted a write. Backups are not encrypted by TillFolder. Use a destination you trust. When you share originals, CSV or PDF reports, you choose the receiving app. Those copies are controlled by that app and the recipient. TillFolder sends no email on its own and does not silently upload your receipt library.

Retention and control

Records remain on your device until removed. Recently deleted items are kept for recovery. Clearing TillFolder's app data or uninstalling it removes its local data; keep an external backup first if you want to retain it. Copies exported or shared to other apps must be deleted in those destinations. A Google Play purchase remains associated with your Google account and can be restored after reinstalling. We cannot remotely access or delete your on-device library. You can change camera and notification permissions in Android settings. Android manages scheduling and may delay or stop background work, particularly after force-stop.

Support and changes

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include. Avoid sending personal receipts unless needed and you choose to share them. We use support messages to answer your request. Changes to the app's data handling will be reflected in this policy before release. Contact hassan@mapifyit.com with privacy questions.
